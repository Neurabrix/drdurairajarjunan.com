Clinician-led technology

Artificial intelligence can support selected parts of endocrine assessment when it is used transparently, reviewed by a qualified clinician, and interpreted alongside the patient's history, examination, and investigations.

This page presents Dr. Durairaj Arjunan's work and interest at the intersection of endocrinology and responsible clinical AI. The current featured application is a clinician-guided bone age workflow.

Featured Clinical Decision-Support Tool

Neurabrix Bone Age Calculator

Bone age is an estimate of skeletal maturity based on a hand and wrist radiograph. Comparing skeletal age with chronological age can help clinicians assess whether a child's bones are maturing as expected.

This comparison can add useful context when evaluating growth, pubertal timing, and endocrine conditions. The Neurabrix tool supports the review workflow with an age estimate, range, and reference images for clinician assessment.

The result is decision support, not a diagnosis. It should be interpreted with the child's history, examination, and other investigations by an appropriately trained clinician.

Responsible clinical use

  • Use AI output as supporting information, not as a standalone diagnosis.
  • Review image quality, clinical context, and any uncertainty before acting.
  • Protect patient privacy and use appropriate clinical governance.
  • Seek specialist assessment when growth, puberty, or hormone concerns require evaluation.

Clinical context

Bone age assessment may form one part of a broader endocrine evaluation. The appropriate investigation and interpretation depend on the individual clinical question.

Plan Your Visit

Prepare for your endocrinology appointment

Bringing a concise medical record can make the consultation more useful. Do not stop medicines or fast for tests unless the clinic has specifically instructed you to do so.

Bring with you

  • Current prescriptions and a complete medicine or supplement list
  • Recent blood-test, scan, biopsy, discharge and referral reports
  • Previous glucose, blood-pressure, weight or symptom records when relevant
