About

Diabetes, metabolism, and hormone specialist at KMCH Coimbatore.

Dr. Durairaj Arjunan is a consultant endocrinologist associated with KMCH, Coimbatore. He completed MBBS at Madurai Medical College, MD in Internal Medicine at PGIMER Chandigarh, and DM in Endocrinology at PGIMER Chandigarh.

His clinical and academic background includes training and work at Madurai Medical College and Government Rajaji Hospital, PGIMER Chandigarh, IPGMER and SSKM Kolkata, and KMCH Coimbatore.

His profile highlights Rank 1 in the INI SS AIIMS and INI SS common merit lists, multiple academic awards, and more than 30 original studies and publications in national and international journals.
