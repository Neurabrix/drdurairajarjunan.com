Plan Your Visit

Prepare for your endocrinology appointment

Bring with you

  • Current prescriptions and a complete medicine or supplement list
  • Recent blood-test, scan, biopsy, discharge and referral reports
  • Previous glucose, blood-pressure, weight or symptom records when relevant
