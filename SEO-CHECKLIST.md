## Required external work

Website changes alone cannot establish local prominence. The site owner should:

1. Verify a Google Search Console domain property for `drdurairajarjunan.com`.
2. Submit `https://drdurairajarjunan.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect the homepage and each service URL, run the live test, and request indexing.
4. Review Page Indexing, Manual Actions, Security Issues, Links, and Performance reports.
5. Create or fully verify the doctor's eligible Google Business Profile, using the exact public name, primary category, phone, appointment URL, KMCH location/hours, and services.
6. Keep name, qualifications, phone, location, hours, and specialty consistent on KMCH, DocIndia, professional-society, Google Scholar, LinkedIn, and other legitimate profiles.
7. Seek a direct doctor-profile link from KMCH and relevant professional or conference profiles.
8. Invite genuine patients to leave honest reviews without incentives, review gating, or scripted claims.

## Measurement

Track Search Console impressions, average position, clicks, and CTR by page and
query every 28 days. Keep one canonical page per intent; expand useful answers on
that page instead of making near-duplicate synonym pages.
